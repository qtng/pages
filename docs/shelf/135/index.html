五愿

Ngũ Nguyện

Five Prayers

5 lines, 100% transcribed

‹ PreviousContents

1
南無一愿大道弘開
Nam mô nhứt nguyện Đại Đạo hoằng khai
ⓘ Firstly, I prayed and determined to open up the Great Path of Thy Third Amnesty.
2
二愿普度衆生
Nhì nguyện phổ độ chúng sanh
ⓘ Secondly, I pray to save sentient beings everywhere.
3
三愿舍罪弟子
Tam nguyện xá tội đệ tử
ⓘ Thirdly, I ask Thou to forgive our sins.
4
四愿天下太平
Tứ nguyện thiên hạ thái bình
ⓘ Fourthly, I pray that humanity in this world can live peacefully.
5
五愿聖室安寧
Ngũ nguyện thánh thất an ninh
ⓘ And finally, I pray for the Holy Land to be secure and the mind of beings without being scattered, crazy, or afflicted, being to be calm.
