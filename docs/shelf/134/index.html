獻仙茶

Hiến Tiên Trà

The Poem of Offering of Tea

4 lines, 100% transcribed

‹ PreviousContentsNext ›

1
梅春月菊味茶香
Mai xuân nguyệt cúc vị trà hương
ⓘ Tea like spring apricot and autumn chrysanthemum, radiates a delicious taste.
2
敬禮誠心獻寶漿
Kỉnh lễ thành tâm hiến bửu tương
ⓘ I would like to offer precious tea with a sincere heart to make a dedication.
3
仰望慈悲加濟福
Ngưỡng vọng Từ Bi gia tế phước
ⓘ I give thanks from Thee for further help for merit.
4
開明大道護清平
Khai minh Đại Đạo hộ thanh bường
ⓘ Opening the foundation of the Great Path of Thy Third Amnesty gives clarity to uphold a peaceful life.
